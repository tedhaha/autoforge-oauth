Privacy policy: autoforge-rclone

Last updated: 2026-09-27

Who uses it

autoforge-rclone is a personal Google OAuth client with a single user: its owner (GitHub: tedhaha). It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses and why

With the owner's consent, rclone on the owner's own computers uses the Google Drive API (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) to upload, list and download the owner's game-project asset files in one folder of the owner's own Google Drive. Nothing else is read or changed.

What is collected, stored or shared

Nothing. There is no server, website backend, analytics or tracking. No data from Google accounts is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone. The OAuth tokens stay in rclone's configuration file on the owner's own computers and are sent only to Google.

How to revoke access

Remove "autoforge-rclone" at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Access stops at once.
