autoforge-rclone

autoforge-rclone is a personal, single-user Google OAuth client. It lets rclone, running on the owner's own computers, keep the owner's game-project asset files (images and 3D models) in the owner's own Google Drive.

It is not a public service. It has one user, the owner (GitHub: tedhaha). Nobody else is invited to sign in, and no one else's data is accessed.
